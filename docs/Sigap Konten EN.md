### The problem we solve

## Chats come in faster than your team can reply.

**Replied to tomorrow morning, the customer has already bought elsewhere.** A customer asks about shipping costs at 11 p.m. The answer arrives at 9 a.m., and by then they've checked out at the shop next door.

**Chats are scattered across many phones.** Staff A and Staff B answer the same question with different prices. When one of them leaves, their conversation history leaves with them.

**Potential buyers go unrecorded.** "I'll get back to you," the customer says. Without a record, nobody knows who they are, what they asked about, or when to contact them again.

**The owner has no overview.** How many chats came in, how many were answered, how many turned into purchases: none of it is visible until someone counts it by hand.

### How it works

## From first chat to loyal customer in five steps.

1. **A message comes in.** A customer contacts your business WhatsApp number.
2. **AI answers the routine questions.** Opening hours, prices, stock, and order status are answered from the information you provided.
3. **Recorded as a contact or lead.** The conversation is automatically linked to the customer's profile.
4. **Staff take over when needed.** Complaints, negotiations, and special cases are handled by people, with the full history right in front of them.
5. **Follow-up and reporting.** Follow-ups are scheduled and the results show up on the dashboard.
